Philosophy

How I Think About Growth

Don't optimize clicks. Optimize economically valuable customer acquisition.

01

Start With The Business

Marketing serves economics. Before opening Ad Manager, I define what we are actually trying to improve (CAC, LTV, Activation, Margin).

02

Understand The Customer

Who is buying, what is the pain point, and what are their objections? Strategy starts with customer psychology.

03

Match Intent With Channel

Does demand exist (Google Search) or do we need to generate demand (Meta/Social)? Channel selection is dictated by intent.

04

Improve The Funnel

Traffic without conversion is waste. Identifying drop-offs between ad click, landing page view, and checkout is mandatory.

05

Optimize Economics

Can the acquisition system sustain itself? If CAC > LTV/Margin, the campaigns must be restructured.

Architecture

The Performance Marketing Framework

I don't start with "Which ad should we run?" I start with "What customer are we trying to acquire, what value are we offering, and what system can profitably convert that demand?"
